FAQ

Everything you're wondering about ClipInd.

What is clipping?

Clipping means turning a business or creator's long-form content into short, engaging vertical clips and posting them to platforms like Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts to reach new audiences.

How do I get paid?

You submit your published clip's link and view count. Once a business approves it as following their brief, your earnings are calculated at that campaign's rate per 1,000 verified views.

Can anyone become a clipper?

Yes. There's no minimum follower count — you just need to follow each campaign's guidelines and use its approved content.

How are views verified?

Views are screened to filter out bots, purchased engagement and other fraud before they count toward a payout, so businesses only ever pay for genuine reach.

Is it free to join as a clipper?

Yes — joining and browsing campaigns is completely free. You just need a public account on the platform where you plan to post.

How do businesses launch a campaign?

Businesses set a brief, a budget, and a payout rate per 1,000 verified views, then publish it — clippers can immediately discover and start clipping.
